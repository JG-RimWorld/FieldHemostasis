## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-25

### Added

- Contextual order for temporarily stopping a pawn's bleeding without tending.
- Per-wound application time scaled by the actor's Medical Tend Speed.
- Progress bar above the patient while hemostasis is being applied.
- Fixed or normally distributed hemostasis duration settings.
- Save/load support for active hemostasis and work progress.
- Support for self-application, injuries, and bleeding missing parts.
